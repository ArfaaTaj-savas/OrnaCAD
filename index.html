⚠️ Known CNC Machining Limits
  • Large gold petal stones are placed by hand and have no holes. Bangles 4 and 15 feature empty cutouts reserved for manual gem setting.
  • The tool places only 1.7 mm stones (SS 4.5), so fine detail such as thin gold wire is not reproduced.
  • Run a test file on scrap material before real production. Check spindle clearance, coordinate origin, and workpiece clamping.
